SMIRK Privacy Notice

Version 1.0.0 · Effective August 27, 2026

This Privacy Notice explains how SMIRK handles information used to provide its AI phone-agent and missed-call recovery service.

Information we process

SMIRK may process business-account information, authorized user information, phone numbers, call metadata, call recordings where enabled, transcripts, caller-provided details, summaries, tasks, workspace settings, and operational logs.

Why we use information

We use information to operate the Service, route and summarize communications, create follow-up records, provide support, maintain security, prevent abuse, comply with legal obligations, and improve service reliability. We do not sell personal information as a standalone data product.

Access and sharing

Authorized workspace users and service providers acting on SMIRK’s behalf may access information as needed to provide the Service. Information may also be disclosed where required by law, to protect rights and safety, or in connection with a business transfer.

Retention and deletion requests

We retain information while an account is active and thereafter only as reasonably necessary for service operations, security, dispute handling, and legal obligations. A verified written deletion request may be sent to alerts@smirkcalls.com. We will review the request and delete or de-identify eligible information, subject to legitimate retention requirements.

Your customer data

Business customers are responsible for providing any notices and obtaining any consent required for their collection and use of caller information, recordings, and text messages through SMIRK.

Contact

Privacy questions or verified deletion requests: alerts@smirkcalls.com.
