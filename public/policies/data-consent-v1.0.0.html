SMIRK Data and Communication Consent

Version 1.0.0 · Effective August 27, 2026

By using SMIRK, a business customer authorizes SMIRK to process communications and operational data needed to provide the Service, including calls, permitted recordings, transcripts, summaries, callback tasks, and authorized text-message activity.

Customer notice and consent duties

The business customer is responsible for determining and implementing all notices, consents, opt-out handling, and disclosures required for its use of calls, recordings, artificial or prerecorded voice, SMS, and customer data. This includes reviewing rules applicable to the customer’s location, industry, and communication practices.

Transactional communications

Where a workspace uses an acknowledgement or callback message, the customer must ensure the message is supported by an appropriate relationship, consent, and disclosure basis. SMIRK may disable messaging where compliance configuration is incomplete, consent is unclear, or carrier/provider requirements are not met.

Data accuracy and authority

The customer represents that it has authority to provide the data loaded into its workspace and to instruct SMIRK to process that data. The customer must not use the Service to send unlawful, deceptive, abusive, or emergency communications.
