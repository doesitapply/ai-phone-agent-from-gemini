SMIRK Cancellation and Refund Policy

Version 1.0.0 · Effective August 27, 2026

Cancellation

You may cancel an active paid subscription through the SMIRK billing-management path or by contacting alerts@smirkcalls.com. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the then-current paid billing period. The Service remains available through that period unless suspended for a permitted reason.

Refunds and proration

SMIRK does not provide prorated refunds or account credits for unused time after service activation or cancellation. Fees already paid are non-refundable except where a refund is required by applicable law or SMIRK explicitly agrees otherwise in writing.

Plan changes

Any approved plan change takes effect as shown in the applicable checkout or billing-management flow. Until SMIRK publishes a different approved policy, Starter usage is subject to its disclosed hard cap rather than automatic overage charges.

Contact

Billing and cancellation questions: alerts@smirkcalls.com.
