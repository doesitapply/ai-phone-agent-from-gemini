SMIRK Support Policy

Version 1.0.0 · Effective August 27, 2026

SMIRK provides account and technical support by email at alerts@smirkcalls.com. The current support target for Starter customers is a response within the same business day. Support is not an emergency-response, dispatch, or 911 service.

What support covers

Support can assist with account setup, workspace configuration, documented product behavior, billing questions, and reported service errors. It cannot make business decisions, guarantee carrier delivery, provide legal advice, or act as a substitute for a customer’s on-call staff.

Escalation

Cameron Church is the named owner escalation contact for SMIRK operational support. Include the workspace name, callback number, and a concise description of the issue when requesting escalation.

Service availability

Telecommunications and AI services depend on third-party networks and providers. SMIRK will use reasonable efforts to maintain service but does not guarantee uninterrupted or error-free availability.
