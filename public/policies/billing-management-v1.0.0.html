SMIRK Billing Management

Version 1.0.0 · Effective August 27, 2026

SMIRK uses Stripe-hosted billing tools for eligible customers to manage payment methods, invoices, and subscription cancellation. Billing credentials and payment-card information are handled by Stripe, not stored by SMIRK.

Subscription billing

Starter is a recurring $197 monthly subscription unless a different approved offer is shown at checkout. Charges recur on the billing date shown by Stripe until cancellation takes effect.

Payment failures

If a recurring payment fails, SMIRK may notify the account contact and limit or suspend paid functionality until the account is brought current. Access is not guaranteed during a delinquent period.

Taxes

For the current first-dollar Starter launch, Stripe Automatic Tax is disabled. This setting is not a representation that tax is never due. SMIRK may update tax collection or billing treatment when a verified tax configuration is established.

Billing help

For billing-management assistance, contact alerts@smirkcalls.com.
