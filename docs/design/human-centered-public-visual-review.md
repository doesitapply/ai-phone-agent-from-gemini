# Human-Centered Public Landing — Visual Review

The compiled desktop landing retains the dark trade-floor visual direction while grounding the product in a recognizable human moment: an electrician works the job while SMIRK protects the business line. The image has sufficient visual weight to balance the purchase/setup panel without displacing the primary buyer action.

The overlaid narrative uses the actual product grammar rather than invented evidence: **Working the job → Signal captured → Context recorded → You decide next**. The copy describes the product mechanism and does not claim that the pictured person, phone event, or callback outcome is real.

The local static preview displayed the expected “Plans are temporarily unavailable” condition because it does not include the production pricing API. This is an honest unavailable state, not a production checkout determination. Live checkout remains separately blocked on a current Railway deployment and end-to-end verification.

At a 390px viewport, the recovery action remains first, the human-at-work story appears immediately below the two primary choices, and the three-step recovery explanation follows. The visual narrative therefore supports the conversion path rather than forcing a mobile owner to scroll past decorative media before seeing the relevant action.
